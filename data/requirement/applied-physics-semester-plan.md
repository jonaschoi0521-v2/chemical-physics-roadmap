## Sequencing this plan respects

**Prerequisite chains**

- **APPH E3100 (Sem 3) → APPH E4100 (Sem 5).**
- **APPH E3300 (Sem 4) → APPH E4300 (Sem 8).**
- **MSAE E3111 (Sem 3) → CHAP E4120 (Sem 4).**
- **PHYS UN2601 (Sem 3) → PHYS UN3081 (Sem 5).**
- **CHEM GU4221 (Sem 5) → CHEM GR6222 (Sem 6).** GR6222 requires GU4221.
- **APMA E4300 (Sem 3) → APMA E4301 (Sem 6).** E4301 is the sequel.
- **MATH UN2030 (Sem 2) → APMA E4200 (Sem 4)** and **APMA E4300 (Sem 3).**
- **MATH UN2010 (Sem 2) → APMA E4150 (Sem 6)** and **MATH GU4032 (Sem 5).**
  GU4032 wants three terms of calculus plus linear algebra; UN1205 and UN2010 cover it.
- No prerequisites listed at the registrar for APPH E3100, APPH E3200, MSAE E3111,
  APMA E4300, CHAP E4120 or CHEM GU4232.

**Term restrictions**

- **Fall only:** APPH E3100, E3200, E4100, E4901, E4903, MSAE E3111,
  MATH GU4032, CHEM GU4221.
- **Spring only:** APPH E3300, E4300, E4018, APMA E4101, E4150, E4301,
  CHAP E4120, CHEM GR6222, CHEM GU4232, PHYS UN1494.
- **Both terms:** APMA E4200, APMA E4300, STAT GU4001, ENGI E1006, ENGI E1102,
  MATH UN2030, ECON UN1105, HUMA UN1121 / UN1123.
