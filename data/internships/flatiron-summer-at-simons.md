# Summer at Simons — Flatiron Institute

**Organization:** Flatiron Institute, Simons Foundation
**Type:** Summer Research Internship
**Status:** identified
**Program Dates:** ~11 weeks, early June – mid August
**Location:** 162 Fifth Avenue, New York City
**Deadline:** 2026-12-01

## What It Is

The Flatiron Institute is the in-house research division of the Simons Foundation —
five centers doing computational science full-time, with no teaching load and no grant
cycle. Undergraduates enter as **Research Assistants**: an assigned project with an
assigned mentor inside one center, not shadowing.

Compensation is $20/hour for undergraduates, with housing support near the offices and
travel reimbursement for non-local interns. Stated eligibility is simply enrollment in a
degree-seeking program.

## Target Centers

**Center for Computational Quantum Physics (CCQ)** — 40+ scientists on equilibrium and
non-equilibrium many-body quantum systems. Its intern brief is "developing and implementing
concepts, algorithms and codes needed to understand quantum mechanical behavior of
many-particle systems," which is close to a description of this roadmap's endpoint.
*Timothy Berkelbach* holds a Columbia appointment and a CCQ research scientist post.

**Initiative for Computational Catalysis** — *Diptarka Hait* joined here in July 2025,
alongside his new Columbia lab.

## Why This One

Both target mentors sit in **both buildings**. The realistic path is not a cold application
in December — it is being known to Berkelbach or Hait through Columbia first, and applying
with an internal mentor who already wants you. Columbia lab first, Flatiron second.

Institutionally it is also the model worth studying: salaried researchers, private
endowment, no publication treadmill, and a culture that builds long-lived scientific
software. PySCF-class infrastructure gets built in places shaped like this.
