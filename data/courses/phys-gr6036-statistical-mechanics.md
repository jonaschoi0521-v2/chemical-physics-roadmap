## Why This Course Matters

The graduate statistical mechanics course, taken in place of CHAP E4120. Statistical
mechanics is the bridge between the microscopic physics and every macroscopic quantity a
molecular simulation predicts — free energies, binding affinities, phase behaviour. FEP+,
the method Friesner's group built, *is* applied statistical mechanics. Learning it at the
graduate level rather than in survey form is the difference between using such methods and
being able to derive and extend them.

Deliberately the hardest option available. Jonas chose it knowing that.
