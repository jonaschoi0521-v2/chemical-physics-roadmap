## Why This Course Matters

Replaces CHEM GU4221 (Quantum Chemistry I). The Chemical Physics major explicitly accepts
either one — the requirement reads "CHEM GU4221 **or** PHYS GU4021" — so this substitution
costs nothing against the degree.

The difference is framing. Quantum chemistry teaches the machinery as applied to molecules;
quantum mechanics teaches the principle the machinery is derived from. Given the stated aim
of building at the level of methods while standing on principles, the physics version is
the one that generalises.
