## Why This Course Matters

Replaces the PHYS GU4021/4022 Quantum Mechanics sequence. The Orgo co-registration
policy in Sem 1 pushed intro physics into sophomore year, which delayed PHYS UN3003
(Mechanics) and UN3007 (E&M) — both hard prerequisites for PHYS Quantum Mechanics.
Quantum Chemistry does **not** require 3003/3007, so it decouples the quantum sequence
from the delayed physics chain.

It is also better targeted: Quantum Chemistry (Hartree–Fock, basis sets, electronic
structure) is the direct foundation of the molecular-modeling / electronic-structure
world (Friesner, Schrödinger) this roadmap aims at — more so than abstract QM formalism.


**Verified against the registrar (2026-10-20):** 4.5 points, not the 3 this file carried.
Instructor **Richard A. Friesner**, who also teaches CHEM GR6222. Prerequisite reads
verbatim "Prerequisites: elementary physical chemistry" - no course number, so it is
instructor judgment rather than a registration block.

Course content per the listing: the Schrodinger equation and its interpretation, exactly
solvable cases, time-independent and time-dependent perturbation theory, spin and orbital
angular momentum, spin-spin interactions, and an introduction to atomic and molecular
structure.

**Preparation plan (decided 2026-10-20).** No research in freshman year; summer 2027 is
dedicated entirely to preparing for this course. Three targets:
- **The quantum half of physical chemistry** - that is UN3080's material, not UN3079's.
  UN3079 (thermodynamics and kinetics) sits concurrently in Semester III but is the wrong
  half for this prerequisite; UN3080 does not arrive until Semester IV, after.
- **Linear algebra past MATH UN2015**, which splits 3pt with probability. Specific targets:
  Hermitian operators, eigenvalue problems, orthonormal bases and completeness, change of
  basis, the spectral theorem, commutators.
- **Quantum mechanics from Friesner's own course materials.**

Note that PHYS UN2601 (Classical & Quantum Waves) is also concurrent, so this is the first
formal quantum exposure on the transcript rather than a follow-on.
