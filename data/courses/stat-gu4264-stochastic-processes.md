## Why This Course Matters

The course APMA E4200 was supposed to be and wasn't. The PDE file argued its way to three
payoffs; the third — score-based generative models — needs **SDEs and Itô calculus**, which
a classical PDE course does not teach. This one does, by name:

> Wiener processes, stochastic integrals, Itô's formula, Girsanov's theorem,
> Gaussian processes, stochastic differential equations

That syllabus maps directly onto three things this roadmap depends on:

- **Score-based / diffusion generative models** — the frontier of molecular generative
  design is written as a forward noising SDE and a learned reverse-time SDE. This is the
  difference between reading those papers and only running the code.
- **Langevin and Brownian dynamics** — the integrators behind implicit-solvent MD are
  discretized SDEs.
- **Kramers' theory and barrier crossing** — reaction rates as first-passage problems.

Placed in Semester VII because GU4203 is a hard prerequisite and sits in Semester VI. Senior
fall is also when the paper gets written, and this is the material that paper most likely
leans on.

**Logistics:** 3pt. Offered fall and spring. Prerequisite **STAT GU4203**; STAT GU4207
recommended but not required.
