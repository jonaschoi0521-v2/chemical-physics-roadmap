## Why This Course Matters

**Waived 2026-11-06 — superseded by MATH UN2010 (Linear Algebra).** Columbia
states that UN2015 "does NOT replace MATH UN2010 as prerequisite requirements of
math courses," and that a student cannot receive full credit for both. Taking it
would have spent the linear algebra slot on a course that unlocks nothing
downstream. The reasoning is recorded in full on the UN2010 file. The original
2026-08-15 rationale is kept below for the record.

---

Replaces MATH UN2010 (Linear Algebra) and STAT GU4001 (Probability & Statistics)
with a single 3pt course covering both — a 3pt saving against the old plan.

Linear algebra is the load-bearing prerequisite for the whole back half of this
roadmap: PDE (Sem IV), Machine Learning (Sem V), the quantum half of Physical
Chemistry, Stat Mech, Quantum Chemistry I/II, and Deep Learning. Probability feeds
Machine Learning.

Settled 2026-08-15:
1. Linear algebra is **not** a Chemical Physics major requirement — so there is no
   named-course substitution problem to resolve.
2. Both downstream uses allow UN2015, including the COMS W4771 prerequisite.
3. UN2015 splits 3pt across two subjects, so it gives less linear algebra than UN2010
   would have. **That gap is covered by self-study** — it needs to be closed before
   Quantum Chemistry I (Sem VII), which is essentially operators, eigenvalue problems,
   and matrix diagonalization. Machine Learning (Sem V) and PDE (Sem IV) come first
   and are less demanding on that depth.

Prerequisite is MATH UN1201 (Calculus III), taken in Semester I — satisfied.
