## Why This Course Matters

The undergraduate thermal and statistical physics course, taken the term before the
graduate PHYS GR6036. Ensembles, partition functions, and the statistical origin of
thermodynamics — the vocabulary GR6036 assumes rather than teaches.

Taking this first is what makes the graduate course survivable rather than merely hard.
