## Why This Course Matters

The algorithms under every simulation. Numerical solution of algebraic systems, linear
least-squares, eigenvalue problems, non-linear systems, optimization, interpolation,
numerical integration and differentiation, initial- and boundary-value problems for ODEs —
all programmed in Python.

Every one of those is a piece of a molecular simulation engine. Electronic structure is an
eigenvalue problem. Molecular mechanics is optimization. Molecular dynamics is an initial
value problem. This plan carries the theory for all three and, before this course, had no
account of how any of them is actually computed.

It also repairs the implementation gap left by cutting the COMS track — but in applied
mathematics, on this field's own problems, rather than in a teaching language.

**Logistics:** 3pt. Offered most terms, usually fall. Programming in Python. Placed in
Semester VII because APMA E4302 requires it as a prerequisite.
