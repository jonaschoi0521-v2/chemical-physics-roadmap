## Why This Course Matters

The whole of multivariable calculus in one term. Vectors in two and three dimensions,
vector-valued functions, partial derivatives, gradients, optimization and Lagrange
multipliers, double and triple integrals, line and surface integrals, vector calculus —
the accelerated version of MATH UN1201–UN1202 for students who arrive prepared.

Taken **instead of** placing out. The math department approved placing out of both
Calculus III and IV on the strength of high-school vector calculus through Stokes' and
the Divergence Theorem; sitting the accelerated course instead trades a free pass for a
graded, rigorous pass over the same material. Vector calculus is the first link in the
chain this whole plan rests on — gradients into E&M, divergence and curl into Maxwell,
surface integrals into flux and into every continuum equation downstream.

Satisfies the Calculus IV half of the major's **Advanced Calculus** path
(UN1202 + ODE), with MATH UN2030 as the ODE half.

**Approved 2026-09-11** by Dr. Vesna Gasperov, Interim Director of Undergraduate Studies,
Department of Chemistry: UN1205 in place of UN1202, and UN2030 in place of UN3027.

Note: a student who takes UN1205 may not also receive credit for UN1201 and UN1202.
