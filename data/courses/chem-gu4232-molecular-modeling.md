## Why This Course Matters

The one course on this schedule where the physics gets implemented rather than derived.

Everything else in the theory core — quantum mechanics, thermal physics, statistical
mechanics, PDEs — teaches how to reason about molecular systems. This teaches how to make
a computer do it. It runs a weekly lab session where the work is building projects with
modeling software, not watching lectures about it.

It also patches the gap opened by moving the quantum sequence from chemistry to physics.
PHYS GU4021/GU4022 give the principles; they do not give electronic structure, force
fields, or QM/MM. Those are the substrate of every platform worth building, and they are
the substrate of Friesner's own group — whose course page lists this class.

Placed alongside PHYS GU4022 in the same term on purpose: quantum mechanics in the
morning, quantum mechanics running on a machine in the afternoon.

**Caveat:** offered **spring, some years** — not guaranteed annually. Confirm it is running
before counting on Semester VI, and keep Semester VIII (also spring) as the fallback.
