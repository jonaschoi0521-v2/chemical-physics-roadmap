## Why This Course Matters

The registered term of research. Everything upstream in this roadmap — the physics
sequence, PChem, the ML coursework — is preparation for producing original work, and a PhD
application is read primarily on research, not on transcript depth. This is where the
transcript starts showing it.

Two structural reasons it sits in the senior year specifically:
- **Departmental honors** requires a 3.6 major GPA *and* participation in research on a
  project of high quality. There is no honors path without this.
- **CHEM UN3920 Senior Seminar** (same semester) draws its presentations from current
  literature and/or UN3098 research. Registering research alongside it makes the seminar
  work off Jonas's own project instead of someone else's paper.

The term ends with a poster at the UN3098 poster session — one concrete, citable output.

UN3098 is **variable credit, 1-4pt**, and repeatable; the department representative must
approve an aggregate above 12 or below 4 points. Registered here at 3pt, which is the
number Semester VIII needs - the variable credit is the point, since it absorbs whatever
the term is short. Entry requires the instructor's permission, and some sections carry a
$105 chemistry lab fee.

**Important:** registering for credit is not the same as starting research. The credit only
fits in the senior year because Semesters I–VI are all 16.5–18.5pt. The lab work itself
should begin sophomore year, uncredited or work-study, and is tracked in `../lab/`.
