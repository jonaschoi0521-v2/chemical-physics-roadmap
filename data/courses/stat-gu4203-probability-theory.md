## Why This Course Matters

The probability half of MATH UN2015 is a fraction of a 3pt course shared with linear
algebra. Everything computational in this roadmap runs on probability at full strength:

- **Monte Carlo** — the workhorse of statistical mechanics and the method under every
  free-energy calculation. Importance sampling, detailed balance, estimator variance.
- **Markov state models** — how long molecular dynamics trajectories become kinetics.
  Transition matrices, stationary distributions, mixing times.
- **Machine learning** — the formal ML track was cut as self-teachable, but likelihoods,
  priors and expectation are not optional background for reading the literature.

It is also the hard prerequisite for **STAT GU4264** (Stochastic Processes), where the Itô
calculus behind score-based generative models lives. This course exists to unlock that one.

Placed in Semester VI so it lands **before** CHEM GU4232 Molecular Modeling and inside the
six semesters a PhD committee actually sees — the application transcript closes at
Semester VI.

**Logistics:** 3pt. Offered fall and spring. Prerequisite is calculus only — one semester,
preferably two, with STAT UN1201 recommended but not required, so MATH UN1205 clears it
outright. A multivariate-calculus review is built into the course.
