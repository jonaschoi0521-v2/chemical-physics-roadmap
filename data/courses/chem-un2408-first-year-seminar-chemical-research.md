## Why This Course Matters

A one-credit, Pass/Fail seminar that introduces research being done inside the chemistry
department and neighbouring science departments. Low cost, high leverage — three reasons
it earns its slot:

- **Lab access is the bottleneck, not coursework.** The registered research credit in this
  plan (CHEM UN3098) doesn't arrive until Semester VII, and UN3098 requires an instructor's
  permission to enter, which means having a lab first. This seminar is the earliest formal
  channel to the faculty who grant that permission.
- **It surveys the field before the field has to be chosen.** Hearing what each group
  actually works on, in first-year spring, is how a computational/physical direction gets
  chosen on evidence rather than on assumption.
- **It costs almost nothing.** 1 point, Pass/Fail, one 50-minute meeting a week. It cannot
  damage the major GPA that departmental honors depends on.

Open to first-year students who have completed at least one term of college chemistry —
CHEM UN1507 in Semester I satisfies that.
