## Why This Course Matters

The final registered term. Applications are already submitted by senior spring, so this
term is not about the transcript — it is about finishing the work: closing out experiments,
getting the paper submitted or revised, and leaving the lab with something complete rather
than abandoned mid-project.

Registered at **3pt rather than 4** because that is the number Semester VIII needs, and
because it keeps the two-term aggregate at 7pt — under the 12 that would require
department-representative approval.

**Logistics:** Variable 1-4pt, repeatable, instructor's permission required. Offered fall
and spring. Some sections carry a $105 chemistry lab fee.
