## Log

### 2026-10-20 - Swapped with MATH UN2030; IV -> II
Frontiers of Science is required of all first-years, and the College pairs it with
University Writing - half the incoming class takes Frontiers in the fall while the other
half takes UW, then they switch in spring. University Writing is in Semester I, so
Semester II is the standard pairing; Semester IV was the deviation.

Corequisite SCNC CC1100 (the discussion seminar) is a separate registration line and is
deliberately not tracked as its own course file here.
