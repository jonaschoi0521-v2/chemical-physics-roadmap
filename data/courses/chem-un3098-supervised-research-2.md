## Why This Course Matters

The second registered term, and the one that sits in the semester that matters most.
Semester VII is senior fall: PhD applications go out in December, and the paper that those
applications are read on is being written right now.

Two reasons this term specifically:

- **CHEM UN3920 Senior Seminar** is in the same semester and draws its presentations from
  current literature and/or UN3098 research. Registering research alongside it means the
  seminar runs off Jonas's own project rather than someone else's paper.
- **Departmental honors** requires a 3.6 major GPA *and* participation in research on a
  project of high quality. Three registered terms across VI-VII-VIII is a far stronger
  showing than one.

The term ends with a poster at the UN3098 poster session — a concrete, citable output
landing in the same months the applications are being read.

**Logistics:** 4pt here. Variable 1-4pt and repeatable; instructor's permission to enter;
the department representative must approve an aggregate above 12 or below 4 points. Across
both terms the aggregate is 7pt, which stays under that ceiling. Some sections carry
a $105 chemistry lab fee.
